# Glossary

Generated by `tools/write_glossary.js` from `src/core/glossary.js`.

Words marked **ours** are this programme inventing a noun where the rule
book uses a phrase. Do not quote them at a sanitarian as though they were
official.

- **adverse pollution condition sampling** sampling done on purpose when conditions are at their worst, scored on the mean and the highest single result
- **approved** water shellfish may be taken from and sold from directly
- **bed** **ours** a named patch of ground inside an area, carrying a tide station and an elevation
- **conditionally approved** approved water with a management plan that shuts it on a named trigger
- **conditionally restricted** restricted water with a management plan that shuts it on a named trigger
- **control season** **ours** the months in which a time to temperature limit applies at all
- **depuration** holding shellfish in treated water under ultraviolet light for a cycle
- **estimated ninetieth percentile** the upper tail of a set, estimated from the spread of the logs rather than counted
- **growing area** a classified body of water, the unit everything else hangs off
- **management plan** the document binding a conditional area to one trigger and one reopening rule
- **most probable number** the count that best explains how many tubes went positive, which at either end is a bound
- **prohibited** water nothing may be taken from for any purpose but seed and relay
- **relay** moving shellfish into approved water and leaving them there for a period
- **restricted** water whose product is legal only after relay or depuration
- **shellstock shipper** a dealer certified to receive, ship and wet store shellstock
- **shoreline survey** the walk of the bank that records what discharges into the water
- **shucker packer** a dealer certified to shuck as well as to receive and ship
- **systematic random sampling** sampling on days drawn without regard to the weather, scored on the mean and the estimated tail
- **the walk** **ours** stepping down the classification table until one class is met, which is how a proposal is built
- **time to temperature** the hours a lot may sit before it must be under refrigeration
- **trailing period** **ours** a stretch of time ending at the moment asked about, with that moment included
- **wet storage** holding shellstock in water to keep it alive, which is not a treatment
